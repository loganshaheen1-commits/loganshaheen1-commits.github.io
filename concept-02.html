01 / The person

A little bit about me.

LS

I'm Logan. I graduated from Loyola University Maryland with a marketing degree, but my favorite part of learning has always been asking why. Why do people choose one product over another? Why do some ideas stick? How can a better process free us to do more interesting work?

I've worked in e-commerce and growth marketing, explored new places while studying abroad in Copenhagen, and learned plenty about leadership as president of Loyola Men's Rugby Club.

Lately, I've been building AI-assisted systems to make my own research, decision-making, and career development more useful. I'm interested in what AI can actually help us do — not just what it can generate.

Outside of work, I like reading, rugby, travel, making things, and conversations with people who see the world differently.
