02 / The person behind the projects

Curiosity is the common thread.

I'm interested in what makes people tick — and what we can make better once we understand them.

I recently graduated from Loyola University Maryland with a degree in marketing. Along the way, I studied in Copenhagen, helped lead our men's rugby club, and spent time learning how brands show up in people's lives.

Professionally, I've worked across e-commerce, marketing research, and growth. The more I learned about those fields, the more questions I had about consumer decisions, the systems teams use, and what technology makes possible.

That's what drew me toward AI. I'm not trying to put AI in front of everything. I'm interested in finding where it actually helps — improving a workflow, clarifying a decision, or making space for more creative thinking.

Outside of work, I enjoy reading, exploring new places, rugby, and conversations with people who see the world differently.
