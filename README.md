## Direction

The site is intended to introduce Logan as a person first, then explore his projects and experiences. The current working design uses a dark color palette with purple accents, a single graduation image on the first screen labeled **Loyola 26**, and a rugby feature/gallery lower on the page.

Other sections cover:
- About me and personal interests
- Marketing and e-commerce experience
- AI-assisted work in progress: Resume Intelligence HQ, Job Discovery HQ, and Operation Slow Burn
- GitHub repositories and ways to connect

Future storytelling may include travel, Copenhagen, and Momentum (men's group), using real photos and verified details.

## Editing approach

1. Keep `index.html` intact until a design is approved for publishing.
2. Iterate in a `concept-XX.html` file.
3. Make small, targeted changes and retain previous versions.
4. Use real photos and accurate descriptions; don't invent metrics, code, demos, or completed outcomes.
5. Confirm responsive layout, accessibility, and links before publishing.
